💾 Safety & backups

Worth reading once. Nothing here is urgent, but it's the difference between a scare and a disaster.

Your backup already exists

During setup, a complete image of your console's internal memory (a NAND backup) was made and verified. If the console ever fails to boot because of a software problem, that file restores it.

It is deliberately not on the SD card. It lives on a computer, because it contains your console's unique encryption keys and must never be shared or published. Ask whoever set this up if you ever need it.

One small file, essential.exefs, is kept on the SD card at /gm9/backups/. Leave it there. It's tiny, and it's what recovers your data if the hardware itself ever dies.

Back up your game saves

Checkpoint copies save files for your games onto the SD card. Worth doing before you experiment with anything, and worth doing occasionally regardless.

  1. Open Checkpoint
  2. Pick a game
  3. Choose Backup

Restoring works the same way, in reverse.

Rules that keep it healthy

  • Power off fully before removing the SD card. Hold Power, then Power Off, not sleep.
  • Don't delete files you don't recognise: especially boot.firm, boot.3dsx, or the boot9strap, luma and gm9 folders. The console won't boot without them.
  • System updates are safe, but there's no reason to install one.
  • Keep a copy of the SD card. Cards fail. Copying the whole card to a computer now and then costs nothing.
If it won't boot: don't panic, and don't keep pressing buttons. A black screen almost always means a missing or corrupt file on the SD card, which is recoverable. Take the card out, check it in a computer, and ask for help before trying anything drastic.

What "bricked" actually means

People use the word loosely. Almost everything that goes wrong on a modded 3DS is a software problem (a bad file on the SD card) and is fixable, often in minutes. Your NAND backup covers the rare case where it isn't.

The genuinely dangerous operations all live inside GodMode9, which is why the menus page says not to poke around in there.

Getting help

The Nintendo Homebrew Discord is the fastest place to get a real answer. Describe what you did, what happened, and what the screen shows.
