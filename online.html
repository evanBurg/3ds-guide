🌐 Online play

Nintendo shut down 3DS online services in April 2024. Two community projects bring them back.

Pretendo, via Nimbus

Pretendo Network is a community-run replacement for Nintendo Network. Nimbus is the app that points your console at it.

  1. Open Nimbus
  2. Choose Pretendo to switch over, or Nintendo to switch back
  3. Reboot the console

You'll need to make a Pretendo account at pretendo.network. Switching is reversible at any time, and doesn't touch your existing Nintendo Network ID.

Not every game works yet. Pretendo is an ongoing project, so check their site for which titles are supported.

NetPass: StreetPass over the internet

StreetPass originally needed you to physically walk past another 3DS. NetPass simulates that over the internet, so you get StreetPass hits without leaving the house.

  1. Connect to Wi-Fi
  2. Open NetPass
  3. Pick a location to "visit"
  4. Leave it running, and hits arrive over time

It works with the StreetPass Mii Plaza games and any title that uses StreetPass.
