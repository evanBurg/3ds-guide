🎨 Custom themes

Anemone3DS changes your Home Menu look and your boot splash.

Straight from the console easiest

No computer needed. Anemone has Theme Plaza built in.

  1. Connect to Wi-Fi and open Anemone3DS
  2. Press L to open the Theme Plaza browser
  3. Browse with L and R, preview with Y
  4. Press A to download a theme
  5. Press START to exit the browser, then A on the theme to install it

From a computer, with a QR code fastest browsing

Browsing on the console is slow; browsing on a computer is much faster. Theme Plaza bridges the two with a QR code on every theme's page.

  1. On a computer, open Theme Plaza and find a theme you like
  2. Open its page — there's a QR code next to the download button
  3. On the 3DS, open Anemone3DS and press R to open the QR scanner
  4. Point the 3DS camera at the QR code on your screen
  5. It downloads straight to the console — select it and press A to install

The console needs Wi-Fi for the download step. This skips the SD card entirely, so it's the quickest way to try a lot of themes.

From a computer, manually

  1. Download a theme .zip from Theme Plaza
  2. Drop the .zip (don't unzip it) into /themes/ on the SD card
  3. Open Anemone3DS and install it
FolderHolds
/themes/Home Menu themes
/splashes/Boot splash screens

Both folders already exist on your card. Anemone also handles badges, the little icons on your Home Menu.

Themes are cosmetic and completely safe. If one looks wrong, just install a different one, or pick the default theme to go back to normal.
