🛒 hShop

Nintendo permanently closed the 3DS eShop in March 2023. hShop is the community archive that stepped in.

There is no official way left to buy digital 3DS games. hShop is a community-run archive that hosts 3DS titles, and 3hs is its app for downloading them straight to your console.

hShop hosts commercial games. Downloading titles you don't own is copyright infringement in most countries. Your call — just know what it is.

Using 3hs

  1. Connect the 3DS to Wi-Fi
  2. Open 3hs
  3. Search, or browse by category
  4. Press A on a title — it downloads and installs itself

It handles updates and DLC automatically, queues multiple downloads, and can keep downloading while the console is in sleep mode.

From the website instead

You can also browse hshop.erista.me on a computer and download a .cia, then install it with FBI. 3hs is easier for anything more than a one-off — see Installing apps.

Installed titles behave exactly like anything else on your Home Menu, including save data and update support.
